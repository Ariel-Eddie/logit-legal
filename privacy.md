# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: May 20, 2026**

LogIt ("the App") is an independent project. This policy explains how we handle your data.

## Data We Collect

- **Workout data**: Exercises, sets, reps, and weights you log
- **Device identifier**: A unique ID generated on first launch for rate limiting and security (App Attest)
- **Account info**: If you sign in with Apple, we receive your name and email (optional) for sync purposes

## How We Use Your Data

- **AI processing**: Your voice, text, and image inputs are sent to Google Gemini API (via our secure proxy) for exercise extraction. Inputs are not stored on our servers.
- **CloudKit sync**: If signed in, your entries sync across your devices via Apple's CloudKit. Data is stored in your private iCloud container.
- **Rate limiting**: Your device ID is used to enforce daily request limits. No personal data is linked to it.

## Third-Party Services

- **Google Gemini API**: Processes text, audio, and image inputs for AI extraction. Subject to [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Apple CloudKit**: Syncs your data across devices. Subject to [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/privacy/).
- **USDA FoodData Central**: Used for nutrition lookups. No personal data is sent.

## Data Storage

- All workout data is stored locally on your device as JSON files
- If signed in, data is additionally synced to your private iCloud container
- No data is stored on our proxy server beyond temporary rate-limit counters

## Data Deletion

- Delete individual entries from the History tab
- Factory reset in Settings removes all local data
- Sign out removes sync but keeps local data
- Delete the app to remove all local data

## No Data Selling

We do not sell, share, or monetize your personal data in any way.
