Your Google account.

Optional Google sign-in connects HeyBlayz to its hidden app-data folder in your own Google Drive.

Google sync is currently being tested in the iPhone preview.

  • App data only. HeyBlayz requests access to its own app-data storage, not your ordinary Drive files, documents or photos.
  • Changes, not database replacement. Sync merges record changes and deletions. It includes saved meals and workouts, catalogs, goals, measurements, conversations, memories, reminders and preferences.
  • You choose the account. Existing local records are copied only with your confirmation. Different Google accounts have separate data.
  • Visible sync controls. Sync runs when opening or returning to the app, or through “Sync now,” with status and errors shown in Settings.

Data and privacy overview

HeyBlayz is an independent app by Ariel Guidi. This overview describes the current preview, including the distinction between cloud storage and AI processing.

Google sign-in and storage

Your Google account identifier selects the account’s local database, and your email identifies the connected account in Settings. Google authorization lets the app read and write its hidden Drive app-data folder. Your sync data goes directly between the app and your Google Drive; it is not stored in a developer-hosted HeyBlayz user database. Device security credentials are not included in sync.

AI processing is separate

When you use Blayz, your submitted text, audio, images and relevant app context can be sent through the app’s proxy to Google Gemini to answer requests and propose actions. This is separate from storing your records in Drive. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

Signing out and deleting records

Signing out stops that account’s synchronization; it does not erase its existing Drive data or its local account database. Record deletions are synchronized, but earlier values can remain in the sync history used to preserve changes and conflicts. Deleting a record is not the same as permanently erasing that history.

About this website
